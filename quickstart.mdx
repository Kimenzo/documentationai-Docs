## Get started

Create your first illustrated storybook in Genesis in a single session, from first sign-in to PDF export.

<Callout kind="info">

You need a modern web browser, a stable internet connection, and a signed-in Genesis account. No installs or design experience required.

</Callout>

## Create and generate your first storybook

Follow these steps from the moment you land in Genesis to your first finished storybook.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Genesis" icon="lock">

    Open the Genesis app in your browser and sign in with your preferred method (email, single sign-on, or social login).

    After sign-in, you land on the main dashboard with options like **Create** and access to your recent storybooks.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Creation Canvas" icon="plus">

    From the dashboard, select **Create** to open the **Creation Canvas**.

    The Creation Canvas is where you describe the story you want Genesis to build. You see fields for your story idea and key details (for example, age range or style).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe your story idea" icon="edit-3">

    In the Creation Canvas, enter a clear description of the story you want Genesis to tell. Include:

    - The main character or characters
    - The setting and time (for example, "a rainy city" or "a distant planet")
    - The overall tone (for example, "funny bedtime story" or "gentle adventure")
    - Any specific moments or lessons you want included

    You can optionally add hints about illustration style (for example, "soft watercolor" or "bold comic style") to guide the visuals Genesis creates.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Start generation and watch progress" icon="play-circle">

    When you are happy with your description, click the button to start generation.

    Genesis begins creating your storybook and displays live status messages so you know what is happening, such as:

    - `Architecting story structure...` while it plans the chapters and pacing
    - `Painting page 3 of 12...` as it creates illustrated pages one by one
    - `Designing the perfect cover...` while it composes and renders the book cover

    Wait until the generation finishes and the **Book Success** view appears. This view confirms your storybook is ready and shows quick actions to view, edit, or share it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Book Success view" icon="check-circle">

    On the Book Success view, confirm:

    - The story title and cover look right for your idea
    - The total number of pages matches your expectations
    - Quick actions are available for **Open in Viewer**, **Edit**, and **Share**

    Use this screen as your checkpoint: if the title or description feels off, you can go back and generate a new version before sharing it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Storybook Viewer" icon="book-open">

    From the Book Success view, click **Open in Viewer** to launch the **Storybook Viewer**.

    In the Storybook Viewer you can:

    - Flip through your story page by page
    - Zoom in on illustrations
    - Access actions like **Download** and **Share** in the viewer toolbar

    Scan through the entire book once to make sure the overall story and visuals match what you had in mind.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Make a small edit in Smart Editor" icon="edit">

    If you want to tweak wording or pacing, open the **Smart Editor** from either the Book Success view or the Storybook Viewer.

    In Smart Editor:

    - Select the page or passage you want to adjust
    - Edit the text directly, or use built-in suggestions to rephrase or shorten
    - Save your changes and confirm they appear correctly in the page preview

    Use this step to fix obvious typos, adjust character names, or smooth out any awkward sentences before exporting.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Export your storybook as PDF" icon="download">

    When you are happy with the story, return to the Storybook Viewer and choose **Download**.

    Genesis prepares a PDF version of your storybook. If exports are not available on your current plan, the Download button redirects you to the **Pricing** or **Upgrade** page instead of starting the download.

    If your plan supports exporting, your browser starts downloading a PDF file you can share, print, or send to readers.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

For your first storybook, keep the idea short and focused (for example, a single adventure or bedtime moment). Shorter stories generate faster and are easier to refine.

</Callout>

## When you hit limits or upgrade prompts

Genesis enforces plan-based limits on features such as exports and some sharing options.

If you try to download from the Storybook Viewer and your plan does not include exports, Genesis:

- Routes you from **Download** to the **Pricing** or **Upgrade** page
- Shows available plans and what each unlocks, including export access
- Lets you upgrade and then return to your storybook to try the export again

Share actions in the Storybook Viewer may show a coming-soon message. When you see this, use exports or in-app viewing instead of direct sharing links.

<Callout kind="alert">

If you are repeatedly redirected to Pricing when trying to download, you have either hit your export limit or your current plan does not include exports. Check the plan details before trying again.

</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="How long does generation take?" default-open="true">

    Generation time depends on story length and illustration complexity. Shorter picture books usually complete within a few moments. While you wait, look for status messages like `Architecting story structure...` and `Painting page X of Y...` to confirm progress.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I change my idea after generation finishes?">

    You cannot directly rewrite the original prompt for an existing book, but you can either:
    
    - Make small text changes in Smart Editor
    - Return to the Creation Canvas and generate a new storybook with an updated description

    Many users generate two or three versions before choosing the one they like most.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if I do not like some of the illustrations?">

    Use Smart Editor and any available visual tools (such as Visual Studio, when enabled in your account) to refine pages. You can also regenerate specific pages or create a new version of the story with a clearer style description to guide Genesis.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why do I see a coming-soon message when trying to share?">

    The Share action in the Storybook Viewer currently displays a coming-soon message while sharing features are being rolled out. Until sharing is available, use exports or show your story directly in the Viewer during reading sessions.

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>
