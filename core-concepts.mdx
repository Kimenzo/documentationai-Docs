## Storybooks and projects

A storybook in Genesis is a complete illustrated ebook: text, pages, and images generated and designed together as one project.

Every time you start a new idea in Genesis, you create a project that becomes a storybook. The project keeps everything in one place: your storyline, scenes, page layouts, and final exports.

<Callout kind="info">
  Think of a storybook as a digital picture book: it has a beginning, middle, and end, broken into pages with matching illustrations.
</Callout>

Storybooks have two key limits that depend on your plan:

- **How many ebooks you create each month**
- **How many pages each book can contain**

If you reach either limit, Genesis prompts you to upgrade or wait until your monthly quota resets.

## Core workspaces in Genesis

Genesis organizes your creative flow into focused workspaces. Each workspace handles a different stage of turning your idea into a finished storybook.

Each workspace works on the same underlying project, so changes you make in one place carry through to the others.

### Creation Canvas

Creation Canvas is where you shape the foundation of your storybook before Genesis starts generating pages.

Use it to:

- Define your **main characters**, setting, and tone.
- Choose the **visual style** you want for illustrations.
- Outline the **core plot** or key scenes you care about most.

When you confirm your setup, Genesis uses this information to architect the story structure and decide how many pages to create within your plan limits.

### Smart Editor vs Storybook Viewer

Smart Editor and Storybook Viewer both show your story, but they serve different purposes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Smart Editor" icon="edit-3">
    Smart Editor is the workspace where you actively change the story text.

    Use Smart Editor to:

    - Rewrite sentences or paragraphs with AI help.
    - Adjust reading level for different age groups.
    - Add or remove details in specific scenes.
    - Ask Genesis to continue or shorten a section.

    When you edit in Smart Editor, Genesis keeps the story aligned with your existing structure so you do not break the overall flow.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Storybook Viewer" icon="book-open">
    Storybook Viewer is the workspace where you experience the storybook as a reader.

    Use Storybook Viewer to:

    - Flip through pages in order and feel the pacing.
    - Check that illustrations match the text on every page.
    - Review the full story before exporting or sharing.

    Switch into Storybook Viewer when you want to evaluate your storybook as a finished product, then jump back to Smart Editor for adjustments.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Review new storybooks in Storybook Viewer first. When you notice something to improve, open that page in Smart Editor to refine the text or structure.
</Callout>

### Visual Studio

Visual Studio focuses on the look and feel of your storybook. While Smart Editor works on words, Visual Studio works on images and layouts.

In Visual Studio you can:

- Regenerate illustrations while keeping the same scene idea.
- Nudge style consistency across the book so characters look the same.
- Adjust the balance between text and image on each page.

Changes you make in Visual Studio update the same storybook you see in Storybook Viewer, so you always review the latest visuals.

## Plans, limits, and exports

Genesis plans control how much you can create and how your exports look.

At a high level, every plan includes:

- A **monthly limit on ebooks** you can generate.
- A **maximum number of pages per book**, which shapes how detailed your stories can be.
- **Export options**, including watermark rules and file formats.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free tier" icon="minus">
    The free tier is designed for exploring Genesis and creating your first storybooks.

    You typically get:

    - A small number of ebooks each month.
    - A cap on pages per book so generation stays focused.
    - Exports that include a visible watermark on each page.

    Some export buttons in the interface lead you to the Pricing or Upgrade screen, where you can see what additional formats and limits are available.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Creator tier" icon="plus">
    Creator and higher tiers are designed for regular use and cleaner publishing.

    With these plans you:

    - Unlock more ebooks per month to support ongoing projects.
    - Increase the maximum pages per book for richer stories.
    - Remove watermarks from exports and unlock formats like ePub.

    When you upgrade, existing storybooks benefit from the new export options and page limits going forward.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="info">
  If Genesis shows a Pricing or Upgrade screen when you try to export, you have likely hit a monthly or page limit, or you are accessing a format reserved for paid plans.
</Callout>

## How AI generation works

Generation is the process where Genesis turns your ideas and settings into a complete storybook with pages and illustrations.

When you start generation from Creation Canvas, Genesis typically:

1. Analyzes your prompt, characters, and style choices.
2. Designs a suitable story structure within your page limits.
3. Writes text for each page.
4. Creates matching illustrations for each scene.
5. Builds a cover that fits the story and visual style.

During this process, you see status messages that show where Genesis is in the pipeline.

### Understanding progress messages

While Genesis is working, you may see messages like:

- `Architecting story structure…`
- `Painting page 3 of 12…`
- `Designing the perfect cover…`

Use these as a live map of what is happening:

- **Architecting story structure…** means Genesis is deciding how many pages the story needs and what happens on each page.
- **Painting page X of Y…** means Genesis is generating or refining the illustration for that specific page. The `Y` value respects your plan's maximum pages per book.
- **Designing the perfect cover…** means Genesis is creating or improving the front cover artwork after it understands the full story.

<Callout kind="tip">
  If progress pauses briefly on a step, wait for the message to change instead of restarting. Restarting generation discards the current attempt and starts a fresh one, which still counts toward your monthly limits.
</Callout>

### What happens when generation finishes

Once generation completes successfully:

- Your new storybook appears in your library.
- You can open it in **Storybook Viewer** to read it end to end.
- You can move into **Smart Editor** or **Visual Studio** to refine text or visuals.
- If your plan allows, you can export the storybook without watermarks in formats like ePub.

Genesis keeps the generated structure so future edits stay aligned with the original flow, even as you refine details.

## Advanced: Regenerating parts of a storybook

<Expandable title="Fine-tuning pages without starting over" default-open="false">

Sometimes you like most of what Genesis created, but one part of the storybook does not feel right. Instead of regenerating the entire book, focus on specific pages or sections.

You can:

- Use **Smart Editor** on a single page to adjust the text while keeping the rest of the story intact.
- Ask Genesis to regenerate an illustration in **Visual Studio** if one image does not match your vision.
- Keep the overall story structure from the original `Architecting story structure…` phase, while only changing a few key moments.

This approach uses fewer generation credits, stays within your plan limits more efficiently, and gives you tighter control over your final storybook.

</Expandable>
